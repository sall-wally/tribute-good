Ten Facts About Batman

Fact Details
First appearance Batman debuted in Detective Comics #27, published in 1939.
Creators Artist Bob Kane and writer Bill Finger created Batman. Finger's major contributions were officially credited by DC in 2015.
Secret identity Behind the cowl is Bruce Wayne, a wealthy Gotham City philanthropist who uses his resources to fight crime.
No superpowers Batman has no superhuman abilities. He relies on training, careful planning, detective work, and technology.
The first Robin Dick Grayson became the original Robin in Detective Comics #38 in 1940.
The Joker Batman's famous arch-enemy made his first appearance in Batman #1, also published in 1940.
The Bat-Signal Gotham's police use a spotlight with a bat-shaped emblem to call for Batman's help.
Alfred Pennyworth Bruce Wayne's loyal butler first appeared in Batman #16 in 1943.
A big Bat-family Over the years, several heroes have taken on roles such as Robin and Batgirl, building a whole family of crime-fighters.
Harley Quinn's debut Harley Quinn first appeared in Batman: The Animated Series in 1992 before joining the comics.
